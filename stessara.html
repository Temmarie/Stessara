Our Story

Made by hand.

STESSARA was born in Lagos out of a love for African artistry and global fashion. Every bag begins as a sketch, becomes a frame, and is slowly dressed in thousands of glass beads — each one placed by hand. We believe the woman who carries STESSARA knows who she is. She doesn't follow trends. She sets them.
